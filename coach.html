Coach Application Form

Applicants are informed herewith that this application process is based on the Circular No. 2009/20 of the Ministry of Education.

All the conditions pertaining to this temporary appointment will be informed to you at an interview to be held in due course. It is mandatory to sign an agreement with the school authorities. Please notice that by submitting your application, you agree to abide by the following conditions:

  • * All Sports Instructors must agree to serve the college as full-time employees.
  • * Once appointed, you must not serve at any school or other institution as a full-time or a part-time instructor.
  • * You must not work at any private academy as a full-time or a part-time instructor.
  • * You must accept that if found guilty of any disciplinary offense, the Principal of Dharmaraja College has the authority to dismiss you at any time without further notice.
  • * If you decide to discontinue your service, you must submit a written resignation letter at least one month prior. Failure to provide notice will result in a deduction of one month’s salary and additional penalty fees.
