SPORTS AND GAMES

Dharmaraja College places a strong emphasis on sports as part of its holistic education. Students represent the school with pride, discipline, and the ideals of humility in victory and graciousness in defeat. The school encourages participation across a wide range of sports, fostering teamwork, resilience, and leadership among its athletes.
