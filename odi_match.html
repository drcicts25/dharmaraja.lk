The Winston Hooles
Trophy

The Winston Hooles Memorial Trophy is one of the most prestigious limited-overs cricket encounters in Sri Lankan school cricket. Contested between Dharmaraja College and Kingswood College, this one-day fixture has been a showcase of cricketing talent from the hill capital since its inception.

Named in honour of Winston Hooles, this annual encounter has grown into a celebrated tradition, bringing together generations of old boys, students, and cricket enthusiasts from both schools.
