Clubs And Societies

Uncover Your Passions Through Ten Distinct Pathways

Welcome to the vibrant hub of extracurricular activity at our school! Our Clubs and Societies page is where students can explore a multitude of opportunities to enrich their academic experience, develop new skills, and forge lasting friendships beyond the classroom. Here, diversity thrives as students come together to pursue shared passions and interests, creating a dynamic tapestry of experiences that complement their educational journey.

Astronomical Society

The Astronomical Society of Dharmaraja College is a pioneer in school-level space science, inspiring students to explore the wonders of the cosmos. Known for its observation camps and technical workshops, the society provides Rajans with hands-on experience in stargazing and astrophysics, maintaining a stellar reputation as one of the most active astronomical hubs in the region.

Library Society

The Library Society of Dharmaraja College is the guardian of the school's intellectual heritage, dedicated to fostering a deep-rooted culture of reading and research.
