The 2026 Annual Prize Giving celebrates the unwavering dedication of the Rajan community to pure academic brilliance. By honoring exceptional scholastic merit—from Grade 5 Scholarship successes to the outstanding O/L and A/L examination milestones that secure university entrance—the ceremony reaffirms Dharmaraja College's enduring mission: forging disciplined, highly educated citizens equipped to be the leaders of tomorrow.

The Annual Prize Giving of Dharmaraja College stands as one of the oldest and most prestigious ceremonies in the nation's history.
