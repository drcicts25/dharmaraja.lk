Admission to Advanced Level (A/L) Classes

Applications are now open for admission to the G.C.E. Advanced Level (A/L) classes of Dharmaraja College based on the 2025 G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) Examination results.

How to Apply

  • Download the application form below and complete the required sections.
  • Send the completed application by post on or before 25th of June 2026.
  • Ensure "Admission to A/L Classes" is written on the top left corner of the envelope.

Postal Address:

The Principal,

Dharmaraja College,

Kandy.

Selection Process

  • Students will be evaluated and selected based on available vacancies and educational qualifications.
  • Selected applicants will be called directly for an official interview.
  • Applicants must attend the interview on the designated date. Those who fail to do so will not be reconsidered for admission.
