A Legacy
Written in Maroon

Since 1893, the Battle of the Maroons has been more than just a cricket match. It is a tradition that binds generations of old boys, students, and families from both Dharmaraja College and Kingswood College.

Played at the historic Asgiriya International Cricket Stadium this year, this encounter has produced some of Sri Lanka's finest cricketers and most memorable sporting moments.
