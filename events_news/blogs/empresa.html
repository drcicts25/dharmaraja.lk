The Annual Commerce Day And Inter-School Competitions

The Commerce Society of Dharmaraja College, Kandy, successfully concluded EMPRESA'26, its flagship Annual Commerce Day and Inter-School Competitions. This prestigious event, aimed at nurturing future corporate leaders, spanned several weeks, with the Inter-school Competitions held from the 2nd to the 22nd of March 2026. The program saw an overwhelming response, attracting over 580 students from 47 invited schools across the island. A total of eight distinct competition categories were featured, including a high-stakes Quiz, Business Idea Presentations, Digital Poster Design, and Case Study & Strategic Competitions in both Sinhala and English, providing a comprehensive platform for over 70 finalists to showcase their analytical and creative talents.

The grand finale and Awarding Ceremony took place on March 26, 2026, featuring a lineup of distinguished guests. The Chief Guest was Dr. Srinath Dissanayake, a proud alumnus of Dharmaraja College and a Senior Lecturer at the University of Kelaniya, whose presence served as a testament to the college's legacy of excellence. Joining him as the Guest of Honour was Mr. Upul Bandara, an expert in the field with a B.Sc. in Accounting (Special) Hons from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura. Their insights provided invaluable inspiration to the young aspirants, bridging the gap between academic theory and professional practice in the ever-evolving world of commerce.

The competitive spirit reached its peak as the winners were announced, reflecting the high standard of talent present throughout the event. Mahamaya Girls' College, Kandy, claimed the prestigious title of Overall Champions, demonstrating exceptional consistency across all categories. They were followed by Ananda College, Colombo 10, as the Overall 1st Runner-up, and Swarnamali Girls' College, Kandy, as the Overall 2nd Runner-up. By successfully organizing EMPRESA'26, the Dharmaraja College Commerce Society has once again solidified its reputation for hosting one of the most significant and influential student commerce festivals in the country.
